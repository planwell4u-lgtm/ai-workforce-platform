# Fictional Retail Sales Demo

**Status:** Internal test content only  
**Date:** 2026-08-27

The companion catalog at
`packages/agent/knowledge/staging-demo/fictional-sales-catalog.jsonl` contains
only invented retail products, prices, and policies. It is not sourced from or
affiliated with any real retailer and must not be presented as production
merchant content.

## Included Demo Topics

- three fictional clothing products and prices;
- a fictional festive collection;
- fictional standard delivery and return-policy summaries; and
- a safe human-sales handoff for custom, bulk, discount, stock, or quote
  requests.

## Safety Rules

- Product names, prices, policies, and availability are demonstrative only.
- The future Sales Worker may answer only from an approved published entry.
- It must not promise stock, delivery dates, discounts, refunds, or human
  availability.
- It must not collect contact, payment, order, or account information.
