# Vendor Management

## 1. Overview

Vendor Management defines the processes and controls used to evaluate, onboard, monitor, and manage third-party vendors that support the Voice Agent SaaS platform.

The platform depends on external providers for critical capabilities including:

* Cloud infrastructure
* AI model services
* Telephony services
* Monitoring platforms
* Communication services
* Security services

Vendor management ensures external dependencies are:

* Reliable
* Secure
* Cost-effective
* Operationally controlled
* Aligned with business requirements

---

# 2. Vendor Management Objectives

The objectives are:

* Maintain visibility of external dependencies
* Reduce vendor-related operational risks
* Ensure service reliability
* Monitor vendor performance
* Manage contracts and agreements
* Support compliance requirements

# 7. Vendor Risk Management

Vendor risks include:

## Availability Risk

Potential service disruption.

Controls:

* SLA review
* Monitoring
* Backup options

---

## Security Risk

Potential data exposure.

Controls:

* Security assessment
* Access restrictions
* Data processing agreements

---

## Dependency Risk

Over-reliance on a single provider.

Controls:

* Alternative providers
* Migration planning
* Architecture flexibility
