# Compliance Operations

## 1. Overview

Compliance Operations defines the processes, controls, and operational activities required to ensure the Voice Agent SaaS platform operates according to applicable security, privacy, regulatory, and contractual requirements.

Compliance operations ensures that production activities maintain:

* Data protection
* Operational accountability
* Audit readiness
* Security governance
* Privacy requirements
* Regulatory alignment

The platform manages compliance across:

* Customer data
* Voice communications
* AI processing
* Infrastructure
* Access management
* Vendor relationships
* Operational procedures

---

# 2. Compliance Operations Objectives

The objectives are:

* Maintain continuous compliance readiness
* Protect customer information
* Establish operational controls
* Provide audit evidence
* Reduce compliance risks
* Support customer trust

---

# 3. Compliance Principles

## Security by Default

Operational processes must include security controls from the beginning.

## Least Data Access

Only authorized systems and users should access required information.

## Evidence Driven

Compliance activities must produce:

* Records
* Logs
* Reports
* Audit evidence

## Continuous Improvement

Compliance is maintained through:

* Monitoring
* Reviews
* Assessments
* Improvements
